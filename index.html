Meet Gogii

Long ago, an adventurer from Lantern Village climbed to Dragon Keep to face the dragon, and lost. The dragon tore the adventurer's heart into ten shards. All that was left is Gogii: a hollow shade with no face and an empty heart. In this dojo, Gogii is you.

Every 100 xp you earn brings one shard back, glowing blue. At 1000 xp the heart is whole, the curse breaks, and you choose who Gogii becomes: Looka, the storm blade, or Suzi, the petal comet.
